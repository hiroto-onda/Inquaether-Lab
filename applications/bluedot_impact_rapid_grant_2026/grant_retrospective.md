# BlueDot Impact Rapid Grant 2026
## Post-Submission Retrospective

The application was completed on 2026-07-15. The requested amount was $400. This retrospective was created after submission and is not part of the submitted application text recorded in `authoritative_submission_record.md`.

## What worked

- The application was based on the existing Prototype Exogram implementation.
- The application presented 370 regression tests passed as evidence of feasibility.
- The application presented the fact that unsafe or authority-related functions are not connected as a safety boundary.
- The research theme was observing how people attribute agency, intent, and capability to AI responses.
- The proposed scope of approximately 20–50 dialogue samples remained small and concrete.
- The proposed outputs were a preliminary observation model, evaluation rubric, and short public report.
- The $400 budget was allocated to API credits, participant or evaluator compensation, and cloud storage, documentation, and publication.
- The application was completed relatively quickly because the existing research, implementation, and public repository were already organized.

## Risks and uncertainties

- The specific method for participant or evaluator compensation has not yet been determined.
- The BlueDot proof-of-concept observation model and evaluation items still need to be designed.
- The evaluator recruitment method still needs to be designed.

- [ ] Design the BlueDot proof-of-concept observation model.

## Reusable lessons

- An existing working implementation and a clear test result made it possible to present feasibility without proposing major new development.
- Explicitly stating that unsafe or authority-related functions are not connected made the safety boundary visible.
- A bounded sample range and named outputs made the pilot scope concrete.
- Because there was no prior participation in BlueDot courses or events, `Other` was selected for the community connection.
