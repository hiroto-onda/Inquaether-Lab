## Purpose

This directory preserves the completed grantmaking.ai Launch Round application as part of the Inquaether Lab Research Memory OS.

It keeps the historical submission separate from later reflection and future reuse:

- `authoritative_submission_record.md` is the immutable historical record supplied after submission.
- `grant_retrospective.md` contains post-submission observations, reusable wording, and follow-up actions.

## Record Boundary

The authoritative submission record must not be rewritten, inferred, summarized, or improved. Corrections or later understanding belong in the retrospective or in a separately dated note; they must not silently alter the historical record.

The retrospective is not part of the submitted application. It may identify reusable material and future improvements, but it must preserve the submitted wording when quoting it.
